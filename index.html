Our mission

About us

Womendetention is a volunteer initiative supporting women held in U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) detention. Many of them are far from home, often without money or a way to reach their families.

We send essentials, books and letters, help women stay in touch with their loved ones and make sure every woman knows she is not forgotten.

What we do

How we help

Three ways your support reaches women in detention.

  • Basic needs

    Funds for commissary, hygiene items and other everyday essentials inside detention.

  • Books & magazines

    We send books and magazines — something to read, to learn from and to keep hope and a connection to the outside world.

  • Family connection

    We help with phone calls and letters so women can stay close to their children and loved ones.
