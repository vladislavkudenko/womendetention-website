Our mission

About us

Womendetention supports women held in U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) detention. Many of them are held far from home, often without money, a lawyer or a way to reach their families.

Together with volunteers, attorneys and partner organizations, we help them get legal support, cover basic needs inside detention and make sure every woman knows she is not forgotten.

What we do

How we help

Three ways your support reaches women in detention.

  • Basic needs

    Funds for phone calls, commissary and hygiene items while women are in detention.

  • Legal help

    We connect detained women with immigration attorneys and help families understand the process.

  • Letters & family

    Letters, visits and help staying in touch with children and relatives outside.
